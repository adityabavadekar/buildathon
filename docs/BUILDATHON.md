# Razorpay AI Buildathon

Source: https://razorpay.com/buildathon/ (fetched 2026-08-28)

Not a prize hackathon — a hiring program. "A student-only program to discover and
hire our next generation of AI Builder Interns." Tagline: *Build. Show. Get hired.*

## The offer

- ₹75,000/month stipend
- 6- or 12-month internship (your choice)
- In-person, Bangalore, starting September
- Shortlisted candidates skip aptitude tests and group discussions, and go
  straight to a panel interview
- No resume screening, no long application

Eligibility: students only.

## Tracks

### 1. AI Growth & Agentic Commerce
Build an agent that grows merchant revenue on Razorpay test-mode APIs, or makes a
merchant transactable by an AI buyer. Money actions must be explainable, bounded,
and gated, with a visible audit trail and one gracefully handled failure.

### 2. AI Risk Manager
A detector, verifier, or auto-responder for one loss category (fraud, returns,
chargebacks). Precision and recall measured on held-out data. Honest metrics
required, including false-positive cost. Strictly defense-only.

### 3. AI Revenue Recovery
Detect at-risk revenue, pick an intervention, run a bounded recovery workflow.
Must show money actually recovered across a batch, plus stopping rules and
escalation.

### 4. AI Finance Controller
Close a finance-ops loop over a batch of 50+ synthetic records, reporting match
rate and unresolved exceptions. "One cherry-picked match proves nothing."

### 5. Open Track
Any domain or problem you choose. "Open doesn't mean easier" — same execution and
depth standards apply.

## Process

1. Choose a track
2. Build something functional
3. Show the work

## Deliverables

- Public repository
- ~5-minute pitch video
- The architecture

## Note on what's actually being graded

All four non-open tracks ask for batch-level evidence rather than a cherry-picked
demo: metrics on held-out data, money recovered across a batch, match rate plus
unresolved exceptions. The differentiator is the measurement harness, not the
agent itself.
